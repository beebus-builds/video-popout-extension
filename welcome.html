Welcome to Video Popout

Pop any video into a floating window, with volume boost and EQ. The extension only runs when you click it — no background tracking.

How to use
  • Click the toolbar button or press Alt+P to pop the playing video.
  • Hover any video for a quick “Pop out” button.
  • Use the popup Volume slider, 125–200% boost, and Bass / Vocal EQ.
  • Tip: Chrome needs a recent page click for the first PiP — click Play, then Pop.
Permissions

ActiveTab lets the button work on the tab you click. Settings sync via your Google account. The extension never collects or shares browsing data.
