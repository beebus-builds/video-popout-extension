Welcome to Video Popout

Pop any video into a floating window. The extension only runs when you click it — no background tracking.

How to use
  • Click the toolbar button or press Alt+P to pop the playing video.
  • Hover any video for a quick “Pop out” button.
  • Tick multiple videos → “Pop selected” for one combined window (Chrome allows one native PiP, so we combine them).
  • “Pop entire tab” mirrors the whole tab; DRM videos may appear black.
Permissions

ActiveTab lets the button work on the tab you click. Optional “all sites” access is only needed if you turn on automatic popout for playlists or tab-switch. You can grant or revoke it in Options at any time. The extension never collects or shares browsing data.
